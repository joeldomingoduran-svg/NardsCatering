NARDS CATERING SYSTEM GUIDELINES


SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Nards Catering is a web based booking and operations system for a catering business.
It has one public customer website and three internal portals for Admin, Manager and Staff.
Customers book events and pay through GCash. Internal users review payments, confirm bookings and manage operations.

Booking status flow: Pending, Confirmed, Completed or Cancelled.
Customers log in with a Gmail account. Internal users log in with a username and password and must pick the matching account type.
Internal sessions last 8 hours.



CUSTOMER


Access
Customer website is the home page. Customer login and sign up is on its own page.


Account
1. Sign up with first name, last name, Gmail address, an 11 digit phone number and a password.
2. Names must be real names with letters only. Numbers are not allowed.
3. Password must be at least 8 characters and include a special character.
4. A 6 digit code is sent to the Gmail address and must be entered to verify the account.
5. Verification and password reset codes are limited to 3 per 24 hours.
6. Forgot password works through a code sent to Gmail.


Booking form
1. Only logged in customers can book. After login the booking form moves to the top of the page.
2. Name, Gmail and phone number are filled in automatically from the account and cannot be edited.
3. Provide venue address, event type, package, event date and event time.
4. Event date must be at least 3 full days ahead.
5. Choose full payment or a 20 percent down payment. The amount due is shown on the form.
6. Pay through the GCash QR code shown on the form.
7. Payment receipt is optional when sending the request and can be uploaded later from the dashboard.
8. Receipt must be a JPG or PNG image, up to 5 MB, and not a tiny file. Other file types are rejected.
9. Terms and Conditions must be accepted. They open as a pop up with a close button.
10. A booking starts as Pending.


Payment rules
1. Payment proof must be submitted within 24 hours of the request.
2. Pending bookings without proof after 24 hours are cancelled automatically.
3. Staff review every receipt manually. Uploading a receipt does not confirm the booking.
4. Refund policy: the 20 percent down payment is not refundable. 80 percent of a full payment is refundable.


Packages
1. Packages show price, minimum guests, assets, dishes and freebies.
2. One package at a time can carry the Popular badge. It is chosen by internal users.


My bookings dashboard
1. Shows each booking with status, event details and payment state.
2. Customer can upload a receipt later for bookings awaiting payment.
3. Customer can request cancellation or rescheduling for Pending or Confirmed bookings.
4. A request needs a valid written reason of at least 20 characters.
5. Reschedule requires a new date and time at least 3 full days ahead.
6. Reschedule is not available on the day of the event or after it. Cancel is still available.
7. Only one open request is allowed at a time. Requests only take effect after staff approval.
8. The notification bell shows booking updates and request decisions.


Other
Availability calendar shows dates already taken by confirmed events.
A chat assistant answers questions about packages, booking and payment.



ADMIN


Access
Admin portal. Log in with the Administrator account type.


Sections
1. Dashboard with booking sales analytics. Filter by weekly, monthly or yearly. Shows total sales, bookings, average sale, best period, status breakdown and sales by event type.
2. Clients for client profiles, booking history and archived records.
3. Inventory for ingredient stock, minimum levels and restocking.
4. Reports for business reports that can be printed.
5. Audit Trail listing every change made by internal users. Filter by user, action and date.
6. Login Activity listing sign in history. Filter by user and date.
7. Users and Settings for account management and the payment QR code.


Responsibilities
1. Create, edit, disable and delete Staff and Manager accounts. Admin accounts cannot be created in the portal.
2. Upload and replace the GCash QR code shown to customers.
3. Create, edit and delete client records.
4. Review the audit trail and login activity regularly.
5. Admin also has full access to bookings, packages, assets, dishes and inventory through the system.


Notifications
The bell icon opens today's and upcoming confirmed events. A red dot means there are events to review.

Use the back arrow at the top left to return to the previous page instead of the browser back button.



MANAGER


Access
Manager portal. Log in with the Manager account type.


Sections
1. Bookings
2. Calendar
3. Inventory
4. Menu Packages
5. Assets
6. Dishes
7. Sales Analytics
8. My Profile


Responsibilities
1. Review bookings and payment receipts. Confirm, complete or cancel bookings.
2. A cancellation always needs a reason.
3. Confirming a booking checks ingredient stock and deducts it from inventory. If stock is not enough the confirmation is blocked and the missing ingredients are shown.
4. Approve or decline customer cancellation and reschedule requests.
5. Create, edit, archive and restore packages, assets and dishes.
6. Mark one package as Popular or remove the badge. This updates the customer page.
7. Add, edit, archive and delete inventory items. Inventory changes are limited to Manager and Admin.
8. Monitor sales analytics.
9. Update own profile photo, name and password.


Notifications
The bell icon opens today's and upcoming confirmed events.

Use the back arrow at the top left to return to the previous page.



STAFF


Access
Staff portal. Log in with the Staff account type.


Sections
1. Bookings
2. My Profile


Responsibilities
1. Review new bookings and check the uploaded payment receipt.
2. Confirm, complete or cancel bookings. A cancellation always needs a reason.
3. Handle customer cancellation and reschedule requests.
4. Create or edit bookings for walk in or phone customers.
5. Archive finished bookings.
6. Keep own profile and password up to date.
7. Staff cannot manage inventory, accounts, reports or the audit trail.


Notifications
The bell icon opens today's and upcoming confirmed events.

Use the back arrow at the top left to return to the previous page.



GENERAL RULES FOR ALL INTERNAL USERS

1. Use only your own account and never share your password.
2. Log out when you finish, especially on shared computers.
3. Verify the receipt amount against the amount due before confirming.
4. Every change you make is recorded in the audit trail with your name.
5. Archive records instead of deleting them whenever possible.
6. Disabled accounts cannot log in.



TECHNICAL NOTES

1. Run the system with npm start. The server listens on port 3000.
2. Database is MySQL. Connection details are kept in the environment file and must never be shared or committed.
3. Customer routes are the home page, customer login and terms. Internal routes are login, admin, manager and staff portal.
4. Uploaded receipts and images are stored in the uploads folder.
5. Gmail is used to send verification and reset codes.
