NARDS CATERING SYSTEM GUIDELINES


SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Nards Catering is a website for customers to view catering packages and request event bookings. Admin, manager, and staff portals support business operations. A booking remains pending until the team reviews it and confirms it.


CUSTOMER

Create and verify an account using your email address. Use the customer login page to sign in or request a password reset code.

Browse packages and submit your contact, venue, event, guest, date, and time details. Bookings must be made at least three full days before the event. Accept the terms before sending a request.

Choose full payment or a 20 percent down payment. Pay using the GCash QR code and submit the receipt with the booking or later through your booking dashboard. Payment proof is due within 24 hours. A booking without proof is cancelled automatically. Sending a request or receipt does not confirm the booking.

Use the dashboard to check booking and payment status. For pending or confirmed bookings, you can request cancellation or rescheduling and provide a reason of at least 20 characters. A request takes effect only after staff approval. A new reschedule date must be at least three full days away.

The calendar shows dates with confirmed events. The notification bell shows booking updates. The chat assistant can answer questions about packages, bookings, and payment.


ADMIN

Use the dashboard to review sales analytics. Admin tools include client records, inventory, printable reports, staff and manager accounts, payment QR settings, the audit trail, and login activity.

Create, update, disable, or delete staff and manager accounts. Admin accounts cannot be created from the portal. Update the GCash QR code used on the customer booking form.

Review client profiles and booking history. Check audit and login records to monitor internal account activity.


MANAGER

Use the bookings and calendar pages to review events, payment receipts, and customer requests. Update booking status to confirmed, completed, or cancelled. Give a reason when cancelling.

Confirming a booking checks ingredient availability and deducts required stock. Confirmation is blocked when required stock is insufficient.

Approve or decline customer reschedule requests and handle cancellation requests. Manage inventory, packages, assets, and dishes. Archive or restore supported records and set the popular package shown to customers.

Use sales analytics to review business performance. Update your profile and password from My Profile.


STAFF

Use the bookings page to review booking details, payment receipts, and customer requests. Update booking status to confirmed, completed, or cancelled. Give a reason when cancelling.

Review and resolve customer cancellation or reschedule requests. Archive finished bookings when appropriate.

Use My Profile to update your own profile details and password. Staff accounts do not have the admin tools for managing users, reports, login activity, or audit records.


ALL INTERNAL USERS

Sign in using your assigned account and role. Keep your password private and sign out when finished, especially on a shared computer.

Check booking details and payment proof before taking action. Internal changes are recorded in the audit trail. Prefer archiving records when the portal provides that option.
