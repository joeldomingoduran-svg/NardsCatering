Terms and Conditions

Version 2026-10-15 · Last updated October 15, 2026

Please review these terms before submitting a catering booking request. By checking the agreement box and submitting a booking, you confirm that you have read and agree to these booking terms.

1. Booking requests and confirmation

  • Provide complete and accurate contact, event, venue, package, date, and time details.
  • Bookings must be made at least three full days before the event date.
  • A submitted booking is marked Pending while Nards Catering reviews the details and payment. A date or availability shown on the website is not a confirmation. Your booking is confirmed only after staff review and confirmation.

2. Payment and payment verification

  • Choose either full payment or a down payment equal to 20% of the selected package price. The amount displayed in the booking form is the amount due for the option you choose.
  • Payment is made through the GCash QR code provided in the booking form. You may attach a receipt with your request or submit it later through your customer booking dashboard.
  • Payment proof is reviewed by staff. Uploading a receipt or sending a booking request does not itself mean that payment or the booking has been verified or confirmed.
  • Payment must be made and proof submitted within 24 hours after the booking request. A booking without submitted payment proof in that period is automatically cancelled.

3. Cancellation and rescheduling

  • For a Pending or Confirmed booking, submit a cancellation or rescheduling request through the customer booking dashboard and include a reason.
  • Requests are reviewed by staff. Sending a request does not cancel or change the booking; the change takes effect only when approved. A requested new date and time are not reserved until staff approves the rescheduling request.
  • The current refund policy shown by the booking system is that a 20% down payment is non-refundable and 80% of a full payment is refundable. Contact Nards Catering to confirm how this policy applies to your booking and any refund arrangements.

4. Booking updates

You can check booking status, payment review, and cancellation or rescheduling request updates in your customer dashboard. Keep the email address associated with your customer account accessible so you can follow your booking.

Questions?

Contact Nards Catering at nardscatering@gmail.com if you have questions about a booking, payment, cancellation, or refund.
